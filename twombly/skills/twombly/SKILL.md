[Twombly](https://twombly.app) puts working screens from your codebase side by side on a canvas. Ask your agent to lay out an onboarding flow, compare two versions of a page, or pull out individual components. You can interact with each frame and share the canvas for comments.

Try asking: “Put our signup flow on Twombly so I can review every step side by side.”

Run `npx -y twombly@latest guide` and follow it.
